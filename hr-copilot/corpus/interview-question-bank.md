# Interview Question Bank

Synthetic examples for structured interviews. Ask the same core prompt and use consistent follow-ups for applicants to the same role.

## Software engineering
Describe a defect you diagnosed. What evidence did you use, what alternatives did you consider, and how did you verify the fix?

## People analytics
How would you validate a report before sharing it? Explain how you would check definitions, missing values, totals, and privacy risks.

## HR coordination
A manager requests a candidate record urgently. How do you verify authority, protect confidentiality, and communicate the next step?
